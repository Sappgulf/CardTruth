Your inspection report

What the images support, and what they don’t.

Includes analysis-size images, not your original files. Keep the originals.

A useful inspection, not a magic grade.

1. Photograph the actual card

Use front and back photos without a sleeve or holder. Avoid glare. Keep all four corners in frame. Do not press, flatten, clean or alter the card for this scan.

2. Confirm the geometry

Align the four outer handles to the intersections of the straight card edges, not the tips of rounded corners. Then align the four inner guides to the printed border’s inner boundary. Suggestions must be checked. For full-art, borderless or ambiguous designs, choose “Skip centering.”

3. Inspect and keep evidence

Review the enlarged corners, use extra lighting-angle photos and mark areas for closer inspection. Automatic bright-spot suggestions can also be glare or normal printing.

What “accurate” means here

Ratios come from the borders you confirm. Their range shows sensitivity to an assumed pixel-placement error; it is not a calibrated confidence interval. A source photograph still has lens, focus, perspective and lighting limits.

No trained grading model, verified authenticity screen, micrometer surface scanner or hardware-calibrated GradeRig is included. LiDAR is not accessed by this browser app. A published centering check is not a prediction of PSA, CGC or BGS results.

Privacy and reopening

There are no network uploads, analytics, logins or automatic storage. The session lives in memory until you save a report. Reopen that JSON file using “Open saved report.” The checksum detects accidental edits; it is not a digital signature or proof of when a card was photographed.

Use a normal browser. A phone’s Files or attachment preview may not run an HTML application. “Take a photo” opens device capture where supported; desktop browsers may show a file chooser. This build does not run a live-view browser camera or a LiDAR stream.
